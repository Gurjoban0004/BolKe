# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Punjabi-speaking families, especially elders who are comfortable speaking Punjabi but may not read or write English fluently. They use BolKe inside everyday messaging, often on smaller phones and in time-sensitive situations such as delivery, payment, and family coordination.

## Product Purpose

BolKe turns spoken Punjabi into familiar Punjabi written in English letters and turns an explicitly copied English message into clear conversational Gurmukhi. Success means the user can understand or send the result immediately without editing, exposing unrelated screen content, or weakening the security posture of banking and payment apps.

## Brand Personality

Calm, familiar, trustworthy. The product should feel like a capable family member explaining something plainly: warm without being playful, confident without being technical, and direct without being abrupt.

## Anti-references

Avoid emojis, decorative controls, excessive cards, technical terminology, tiny labels, weak contrast, hidden primary actions, ornamental motion, and any interaction that asks for broad access to screen content. BolKe must not resemble a novelty keyboard, a developer console, or an overlay utility.

## Design Principles

1. Put speaking or understanding one tap away.
2. Earn trust through visible limits: request only the current audio or copied text and explain when it leaves the device.
3. Make the safest behavior automatic in password, PIN, OTP, and payment fields.
4. Prefer natural family language over literal or literary translation.
5. Keep recovery obvious: every temporary mode has a clear route back to typing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, use Android system typography and icons, provide at least 48dp touch targets, strong focus states, large readable Gurmukhi, and no decorative animation. Design for elders, variable literacy, reduced dexterity, and use in bright ambient light. Never rely on color alone to communicate state.
